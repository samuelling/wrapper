What the bones hide

    The palaeo parallel

    The idea comes from All Yesterdays (2012) by John Conway, C. M. Kosemen and Darren Naish, which drew modern animals as if they had been reconstructed from bones alone. The skeletons here are simplified side-view diagrams drawn for this page, not traced from specimens. The shrink-wrap stage is computed: skin is pulled tight over the bones, bridges small gaps and sags into every hollow.
