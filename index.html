What the bones hide

    The palaeo parallel

    After All Yesterdays (2012) by John Conway, C. M. Kosemen and Darren Naish, which drew living animals as if reconstructed from bones alone. The skeletons are simplified models built for this gallery. The shrink-wrap is computed: skin is pulled tight over the bones, spans small gaps and sinks into every hollow. The curator’s models are invented misreadings, in the spirit of the book’s closing section, where living animals are restored by people who know only their bones. Animals are shown at real scale. You can also see them as flat side-view plates.
